The first industry talk was titled From Developer to CEO – The Perfect Blend of Technology and Business. It was held on 26 October 2026, from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM. The session was delivered by Professor Dr. Haji Muhammad Hafiz Izran bin Ishaq, who is the CEO of ART Mechatronics PLT and a lecturer at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, UTM. Since this live session clash with my class schedule, I was unable to join the live, so I watched the official recorded webinar instead to understand the discussion and gain knowledge from the speaker's sharing. After that, I worked with my groupmates to summarise the main points and present it in a poster.

One of the main points I gained from the talk was the importance of connecting technical development with business value. As a computer science student, I usually focus on writing code and developing systems that function properly. However, Dr. Hafiz explained that developing a successful product also requires us to consider whether the system solves a real problem and provides value to customers. This made me realise that technical skills alone are not enough to build a successful technology business.

Next, I learned about the concept of Minimum Viable Product (MVP) where developers do not need to complete every feature before launching a product. Instead, they can start with a basic version that includes essential functions, such as a QR-based visitor management module that was introduced by the speaker. Then, the developers can use the feedback received from users to identify areas that require further improvement. I find this approach was useful because it allows developers to test their ideas and improve the system based on actual user experiences.

I also learned about the role of AI tools in modern software development. Tools such as ChatGPT and AI can help developers generate ideas and improve their workflow. However, I also understand that AI should only be used as an assistant rather than a replacement for human developers. Developers are still responsible for validating the accuracy of generated code and maintaining system reliability. This makes me realized that human judgement and technical knowledge remain important even with the increasing use of AI.

What I Gained & Personal Reflection

After watching this session, I started to see technology development from a different perspective. Before this, I usually focused more on making sure the code worked properly and gave the correct output. However, after listening to Dr. Hafiz experiences as technology company leader, I realised that developing a successful system involves more than just coding. There are other things that need to be considered, such as gaining users' trust, managing costs for cloud services and hardware maintenance, and understanding the differences between end-users and paying customers. From this talk, I realised that having good technical skills is important, but it is also necessary to understand the business side of technology. I also learned that, as a student, I can already start exploring business ideas and apply the knowledge I have gained through my studies to develop useful solutions.

For my future software development projects, I want to improve the way I plan and approach a project before starting the actual development. I will first identify the and understand the requirements of the people who will be using the system. I also want to consider whether the system will be practical and useful in real-life situations, rather than focusing only on completing the technical requirements of an assignment. Besides that, I plan to improve my understanding of project management, system maintenance and cost considerations. I believe these aspects are important because developing a system does not end after the coding process is completed. The system also needs to be maintained and updated to ensure that it continues to meet users' needs. By improving these areas, I hope to become more confident in developing systems that are not only technically functional but also useful and reliable.
